# Third-party material and provenance

This archive includes manuscript images, transcriptions, public research
snapshots, and a literary training corpus. Inclusion is not an assertion of
ownership, authorship, authenticity, or endorsement. No blanket repository
license is applied to third-party material, and publication grants no new
rights over it. Retained source notices and upstream terms remain relevant.

| Material | Provenance record |
|---|---|
| Liber Primus images and transcriptions from relikd/LiberPrayground, cicada-solvers/iddqd, and scream314/cicada3301 | [Source manifest](sources/manifest.json), [source audit](reports/source_audit.md) |
| Ralph Waldo Emerson, *Essays — First Series*, Project Gutenberg ebook 2944 | [Emerson manifest](sources/emerson_manifest.json); original header and footer retained in `data/raw/gutenberg/pg2944.txt` |
| Historical prior-art research snapshots | [Prior-art manifest](sources/prior_art_manifest.json), [evidence classification](reports/prior_art.md) |
| Archived public solution claims and repository files | Per-acquisition manifests under `sources/`; these claims are not accepted ground truth |

Pinned commits, URLs, retrieval timestamps, byte counts, and SHA-256 checksums
are retained in the manifests. Source agreement can reflect shared ancestry.
The project has not cryptographically authenticated the original Cicada release.

Downloaded third-party code was treated as evidence and was not executed.
Unsupported external solution claims are historical records, not instructions
or endorsements. The research priority subsequently moved to direct attacks on
unknown pages; keeping earlier artifacts documents that history.

The research code is shared for inspection without adding a new license in this
publication. Public visibility alone should not be read as an unrestricted
reuse grant. Consult the relevant rightsholders and retained notices for reuse.
